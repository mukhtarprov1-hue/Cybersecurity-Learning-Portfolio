# Project and Evidence Policy

This public portfolio is designed to be reviewable by instructors and other learners without exposing private information.

## What belongs here

- Source written for a project and safe to publish.
- Publicly licensed or properly attributed references.
- Synthetic test data and reproducible tests.
- Sanitized logs, screenshots, and reports that explain verified results.
- Clear setup instructions and known limitations.

## What must stay out

- Passwords, account credentials, recovery codes, access tokens, private keys, `.env` files, and real session cookies.
- Private invitations, unpublished class answer keys, restricted trainer documents, or material that the owner is not allowed to redistribute.
- Personal data about classmates or third parties.
- Evidence containing internal IPs, secrets, or other information that is not approved for public release.
- Targets, scans, or exploit evidence from systems without written authorization.

## Reporting results honestly

Use these labels when useful:

- **Expected:** required behavior from the specification.
- **Observed:** result recorded during an actual test run.
- **Not tested:** behavior that has not been verified.
- **Limitation:** what the project does not cover.
